Add to Samsung Galaxy S26+ Home Screen

Android requires a web app to be opened from an HTTPS URL (like free GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, or Vercel) to install as a full-screen standalone app:

  1. Download the household_budget_pwa.zip bundle and unzip it (or upload the unzipped folder to app.netlify.com/drop in 10 seconds).
  2. Open that HTTPS link on your Galaxy S26+ in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  3. Tap Install App here (or tap the browser menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install).

Once installed, it launches full-screen without an address bar and saves all your bills and logged expenses offline in IndexedDB.
